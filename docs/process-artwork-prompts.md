# HOW WE WORK — The HUGs Journal

Three decorative artworks generated with the built-in image_gen tool: New York editorial style, ivory newsprint, serif type, monochrome photos and HUGs pink stamps. Fictional journal artwork, not a real publication or studio location claim.

Final assets in public/media/process: brief-journal.webp, scope-journal.webp, deliver-journal.webp. Exported as WebP quality 88 without cropping. Original generated PNG files retained at the generator output paths.

## Prompts

### brief

Create ONE standalone portrait editorial newspaper front page, aspect ratio 3:4, flat front-facing full-bleed print artwork, all four edges visible, no surrounding scene, no perspective, no mockup background, no hands holding the page. A sophisticated independent New York creative journal called "The HUGs Journal" (original masthead, do NOT use the New York Times name or logo). Off-white ivory newsprint with subtle natural paper grain, dense yet elegant black serif newspaper typography, thin black rules, a bold editorial headline, tiny all-caps section labels, two or three neat columns. One striking black-and-white halftone photograph occupies about 40% of the page, a small secondary editorial image or diagram, a tiny restrained magenta #EB2266 issue stamp. Realistic printed ink, beautiful restrained art direction, readable short text and generous 6% margins. This is one of a matching three-page creative agency process series. Secondary small photo: an architectural detail in Da Nang. Three short column headings "THE GOAL", "THE REFERENCES", "THE TIMELINE", each with one concise relevant English sentence. Footer "HUGs Creative — Born in Da Nang. Driven by Creativity." A circle around handwritten "Start here" in magenta. Composition has headline across top, photograph at center, columns at bottom.
